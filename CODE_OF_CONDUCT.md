# Code of conduct

PlantCraft follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, criticise the work and not the person,
and keep plant and company details that are not yours to share out of issues and discussions.

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks and publishing someone's private
information are not accepted, in the repository, its discussions or any space that speaks for
the project.

**Reporting.** Write privately to the maintainers at
[info@plantcraft.io](mailto:info@plantcraft.io). Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept confidential. The maintainers may
edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and ban someone from the
project for repeated or serious breaches, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
