You may, without asking:

- say that your work uses, runs on, or is compatible with PlantCraft, in plain text;
- name PlantCraft in articles, talks, courses and comparisons;
- run unmodified official bundles under their own names.

Please ask first before you:

- publish a modified version, a fork or an app under a PlantCraft name or logo (rename it, and
  say it is based on PlantCraft);
- use a PlantCraft name or logo in a product, company or domain name, or in a way that suggests
  the project endorses you.

Questions: open a discussion, or write to the maintainers at
[info@plantcraft.io](mailto:info@plantcraft.io).
