# An asset in Analytics

One PlantCraft asset as Analytics sees it: the result of every algorithm that runs on it (the same
columns as Results), then the data quality of its signals by role (PV, OP, SP...), the worst
result first. Open a result for its series and findings; **Loop analysis** opens the loop's
full analysis when the asset is a control loop.

An asset no algorithm runs on says so, with a way to monitor one. Analytics only reads the asset;
its name, signals and roles are changed on PlantCraft's Assets page.
