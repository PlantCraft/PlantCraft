01 · Open

All of it open, and yours to extend.

Every line of code and every method is open. Nothing is a black box: each result says how it was reached, in blocks and Python you can read, change and share.

  • Apache-2.0Use it, change it, ship it inside your own products. No paid edition, no locked feature.
  • Open methodsThe performance and data quality methods are open and written in Python, from public literature, so you can check, cite and improve them.
  • A complete suiteApps for data, analytics, alarms, dashboards, models and documents, ready to install and configure, sharing the same people, assets, signals and look.
  • ExpandableNew apps for your process, your research or your client are created from a template and slot in beside the official ones.

02 · Secure

Built for the plant's IT and OT review.

Plants install nothing their security teams have not approved. PlantCraft is built for that review from the first line, and a two-page security guide maps it to the Purdue model and IEC 62443.

  • On your own infrastructureInstalls on your servers and inside your network, from one signed bundle, even with no internet. No telemetry, licence call or outside service.
  • Read-only to the plantReads OPC UA, PI Web API, MQTT, Modbus TCP, SQL and CSV. It never writes to a DCS, PLC or historian.
  • Protected by defaultOne HTTPS port with sign-in and an audit log, calculations isolated with no network or secrets, and a backup before every update.
  • AI you configureOff until an administrator turns it on. Connect the provider you choose, or a model your company already runs. The assistant reads only what the person asking may read.

03 · LLM-first

An assistant that finds the problems with you.

Instead of searching through results and charts, ask. The assistant checks the calculations and the results, points to what needs attention and suggests what to do, so your time goes to understanding the problem and planning the fix with your team.

  • Checks the resultsIt reads the plant's data, the results of every app, its documents and history, and answers with charts and links to its sources.
  • Suggests actionsIt says what is wrong, why, and what to do first. A person always confirms.
  • Helps you build, tooIt proposes new blocks, algorithms, alarm rules and models with their fields filled in, for you to review.
  • Your own AI, tooCopy any block, model or the plant map as a prompt, or let your assistant read the plant through a read-only MCP server.

Install

One bundle, one command.

Made for networks without internet: copy the bundle to the server, check its signature, run the installer. It sets up the platform, the apps, the demo plant if you want it, and an administrator account.

Linux for plant servers; macOS and Windows with Docker Desktop for trials. The install guide · Requirements

# Linux with Docker; the bundle from the releases page
tar -xzf %%BUNDLE%%.tar.gz
cd %%BUNDLE%%
./install.sh --all-apps --demo
# open https://<server>/ and sign in as admin
