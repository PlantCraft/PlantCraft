has a row here (AGENTS.md, "Clean room"). The PlantCraft logo and mark are
original work of the PlantCraft authors (Apache-2.0, see `docs/brand.md`).

## Data

All demo data is synthetic, generated by the Simulator (`simulator/`) or by the test server in `tests/opcua_plant.py`. No client or
employer data is included.
